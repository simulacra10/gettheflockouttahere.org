## The technology

[Flock Safety](https://www.flocksafety.com/) sells automatic license plate reader (ALPR) cameras to local
governments and police departments. Each camera photographs every vehicle
that passes, extracts the plate number, and logs the vehicle's make, color,
type, and distinguishing features (bumper stickers, roof racks, damage) using
image recognition — not just the plate.

## What gets captured

- License plate number
- Date, time, and precise GPS location of the read
- Vehicle characteristics (make, color, type)
- A photograph of the vehicle

This happens for every vehicle, regardless of whether it's linked to any
investigation.

## Where the data goes

Flock operates a shared network model: agencies that deploy Flock cameras can
opt to share read data with other agencies on the network, including
out-of-state and federal agencies, independent of a warrant. Retention periods
and sharing settings are configured per agency and are not always public by
default.
