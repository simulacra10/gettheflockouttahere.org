You read that right, folks. Easton had four ALPR cameras. Now we are back to three.  

Last week the family and I met up with some relatives in OC for a few days of fun in the sun. I highly recommend the Holiday Inn on 66th Street. Nice clean rooms, saltwater pool with swim-up bar, and the playlist was amazing. Lots of reggae, ska, Southern rock, and even some metal AI remakes.

By Wednesday morning I had no less than 5 messages from different sources telling me that a new Flock camera went up by Walmart. My initial thought, and everyone else's, was the same: Walmart must have put it up. However, when we came home Friday, I had the chance to go see the camera for myself and do a little filming as well. By the time I arrived, someone had vandalized it.

By Tuesday, August 25, the camera was taken down.
