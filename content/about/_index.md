DeFlock Easton is an independent community project, not affiliated with the
Town of Easton, Talbot County government, or any official body. It exists to
document the Flock Safety ALPR cameras already installed in Easton with
sourced facts, and to help residents organize for their removal.

## Credibility policy

Every factual claim on this site — camera counts, timelines, quotes — links
to a source. When a claim turns out to be wrong or incomplete, it is
corrected publicly in the [changelog](/changelog/) rather than silently
edited. See `ARCHITECTURE.md` in the site repository for the full policy.
